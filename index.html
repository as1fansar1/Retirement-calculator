Methodology — Nest egg calculated using the present value of an inflation-adjusted annuity (safe withdrawal rate model). S&P 500 returns are nominal total returns (dividends reinvested). Withdrawals are adjusted annually for inflation. Monthly savings use future value of annuity formula. The Monte Carlo section simulates 1,000 retirement paths, sampling annual returns from a normal distribution (Box-Muller transform) and tracking portfolio survival across the full retirement period.
